Durness and Kinlochbervie Angling Club

North West Sutherland, Scotland

The club holds fishing rights on a number of lochs in the North West of Sutherland, from the Cape Wrath Parph, down past Oldshoremore and out to Rhiconich. Club permits also extend to unnamed hill lochs beyond those listed.

  • Lake
  • Brown trout, Salmon & Sea trout

Permission & Tickets

Permission & Tickets

Visitor permits are available for anglers who wish to fish the club's waters without full membership. These permits offer access to well-maintained fishing grounds in a quiet, scenic setting and help support the ongoing conservation and management of the fishery.

Both experienced anglers and newcomers are welcome. By purchasing a permit, visitors are able to enjoy a day's fishing while contributing to the care and sustainability of the waters.

Fishery rules

Byelaws
  • Only one rod may be used at a time per angler
  • No Sunday fishing for salmon and sea trout — Scots law, all waters
  • Follow the Scottish Outdoor Access Code
  • Do not disturb ground-nesting birds
Fish limits & sizes
  • Brown trout minimum size 10 inches (25 cm)
  • Bag limit of four brown trout per angler per day
  • All salmon and sea trout must be returned alive
  • Record your catch with the club after each visit
Permitted methods
  • Fly fishing only on lochs holding salmon and sea trout
  • Fly and spinning permitted on brown trout lochs
  • Worm fishing discouraged — deep-hooked fish rarely survive release
  • Single barbless hooks encouraged when releasing fish
Protected wildlife

Ground-nesting birds

North West Sutherland is an important breeding ground for many bird species, and it is a criminal offence to disturb nesting wild birds. Many nest on the ground close to the lochs — take care on the way to and from the water, and avoid disturbance while fishing.

You may come across red-throated divers on some of the smaller hill lochs. If so, simply move on to another loch: an adult bird kept off the nest leaves its eggs and young exposed to predators.

Biosecurity

Check, clean and dry waders, nets and boats before moving between waters, especially if you have fished outside the area recently. Invasive weeds can spread from the smallest fragment of stem.

Seasonal notices

July 2026 — divers are nesting on the small islands of Loch na Claise Càrnaich. Please avoid the loch until the young have fledged.

Rules for individual lochs — boat limits, fly-only restrictions, access conditions — appear on each water's own page.
