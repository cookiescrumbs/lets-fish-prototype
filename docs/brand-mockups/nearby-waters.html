Loch Lomond & The Trossachs

8 waters within 50 miles

Map view — Leaflet topo map with clustered markers (see components catalogue)
1

River Falloch

Crianlarich, Stirling

A spate river tumbling down Glen Falloch to the head of Loch Lomond — strong runs of salmon and sea trout after rain.

River Salmon & sea trout
2

Loch Ard

Aberfoyle, Stirling

A scenic Trossachs loch under Ben Lomond, well stocked with wild brown trout and pike among its wooded bays.

Loch Brown trout & pike
3

Loch Chon

Kinlochard, Stirling

A quiet hill loch on the Loch Katrine road, holding hard-fighting wild brownies that rise freely to the dry fly.

Loch Brown trout
4

Lake of Menteith

Port of Menteith, Stirling

Scotland's only "lake" and a renowned stillwater rainbow fishery, fished from boats over open water and quiet shallows.

Stillwater Rainbow trout
5

River Teith

Callander, Stirling

A classic fly river draining the Trossachs lochs, with consistent salmon and sea trout and free-rising resident brownies.

River Salmon & sea trout
6

Loch Venachar

Brig o' Turk, Stirling

A large Trossachs loch open to the wind, giving boat anglers a spirited mixed bag of brown trout, perch and pike.

Loch Brown trout, perch & pike
7

Loch Lubnaig

Strathyre, Stirling

A steep-sided loch in the Pass of Leny with easy bank access, holding brown trout, the odd salmon, and pike in the margins.

Loch Brown trout & pike
8

River Leven

Balloch, West Dunbartonshire

The short, powerful outflow of Loch Lomond to the Clyde — one of Scotland's most productive salmon and sea-trout rivers.

River Salmon & sea trout
