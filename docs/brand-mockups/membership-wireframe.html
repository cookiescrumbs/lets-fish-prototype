Memberships — wireframes

A membership is a ticket for a club's season. Andy sets up "D&KLB membership" at £90 for the 2027 season, with room for 150 members. Callum buys one in his own name. Grey boxes only.

Agreed: a season is a year, not a pair of dates. The membership says "2027 season" and nothing more. When the fishing actually opens and closes is set by the fishery's own seasons and rules, which already show on its page. The membership doesn't repeat them, so each club stays flexible.

Agreed: full price whenever you join. Callum pays £90 for the 2027 season whether he joins in March or August.

Agreed: a membership can have a cap on numbers. Andy sets the most members the club takes in a season. Once it's reached, the card says the season is full.

Agreed: one membership per club, in your own name. Step 1 shows Callum's name from his account rather than asking for an angler. He can hold one membership per club per season. If D&KLB sells an adult and a junior membership, he can have one or the other, not both. Next season goes on sale on 1 January, so he renews then.

Agreed: Ewan pays after he's approved. Nothing is charged when he applies. Approval emails him a link to pay, which opens the usual card step.

Agreed: a place is only taken once he's approved and paid. Applying and being approved don't use one up. If the 30 junior places fill while he's waiting, his pay link says the season is full.

Agreed: an approval runs out after 14 days unpaid. After that he has to apply again.

Agreed: the application has a free-text box. It's labelled "Anything the club should know", for example "Proposed by John Ross". That covers clubs that need a recommendation from an existing member.

Agreed: Andy can give a reason when he declines. It's optional, and it goes in the email Ewan gets: "Unfortunately you've not been successful…"

Agreed: Andy gets one email per application. Each links to the Applications page, a page of its own reached from the account menu and from a notice on his fishery page. If 30 emails a week gets annoying, a daily summary can come later.

Agreed: memberships get their own page. Your Memberships sits under Your Tickets in the account menu for every signed-in angler. Memberships leave Your Tickets completely, which keeps day and week tickets.

Agreed: the season changes by itself on 1 January. From 1 January 2028 the membership sells the 2028 season, with nothing for Andy to do. Someone who joins on 20 November 2027 gets the 2027 season, and the card and step 1 both say "2027 season", so he can see what he's buying. For "Current" and "Past", a season runs 1 January to 31 December. Callum never sees those dates.

Agreed: the cap is the total number of members in a season. It starts again from zero on 1 January. Someone partway through paying keeps their place for a few minutes, as with a day ticket.

Agreed: the button depends on how the club admits members. It says Buy for a club that sells to anyone while there's space, and Apply for one that vets members. Only Buy works in this round.

Agreed: the card shows how many places are left. It says "12 places left", the way a day ticket says "4 left today".

Agreed: a club can sell several kinds of membership. For example adult, junior, local and visitor. Each is its own ticket with Duration set to Membership, with its own price and waters. One membership per club still applies, so Callum holds a local membership or a visitor one, never both.

Agreed: each kind of membership has its own cap. Andy sets 100 adult places and 30 junior places, and juniors can fill up while adult places are still left.

Agreed: a membership can need approving. Andy ticks "Applicants need approving" on the membership. The button then says Apply instead of Buy. Callum sends his details, Andy is told and approves or declines, and Callum pays once he's approved. His date of birth, address and phone are saved to his account, so the next form is already filled in.

No open questions. Everything below is agreed.
