Loch Coruisk

Water managed by Skye & Lochalsh Angling Association

Type
Loch
Species
Brown trout
High alkalinity
Shallow
Low shoreline complexity
Mid elevation
Very small

Cradled beneath the Black Cuillin, Loch Coruisk is one of Skye's most dramatic stretches of water — a long, dark freshwater loch reached only on foot or by boat across Loch Scavaig. The walk in rewards you with brown trout in a setting few other waters can match.

The fish run small but free-rising, and the loch fishes best on an overcast day with a light ripple. Wading is straightforward along the northern shore; the south side is rockier and better covered from the bank.

Rules for this loch

Permitted methods
  • Fly fishing only — no spinning or bait
Byelaws
  • Wading is discouraged along the south shore; deep drop-offs close to the bank
Seasonal notices
  • No fishing east of the outflow while divers are nesting on the islands

See all Skye & Lochalsh AA rules
Permission & tickets

Day tickets are available from the association at £12, covering all club waters. No Sunday fishing for salmon and sea trout.

Boats

One association boat is kept at the north end. Book through the secretary; oars and bailer provided, bring your own buoyancy aids.
