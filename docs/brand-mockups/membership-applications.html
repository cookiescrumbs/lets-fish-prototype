Mockup note: ship 2 of memberships, applications. D&KLB's Junior membership (under 16) needs approving. On Wed 3 Mar 2027 Ewan Mackay, 14, applies. Andy approves him, and Ewan pays £20 and joins. Priya Shah, who lives in Inverness, applied for the Local membership the day before, and Andy declines her. Each panel shows the desktop page and then the same page on a phone.

Ship 2 decisions this mockup makes

The plan's Ship 2 section is agreed. These are the gaps the wireframe left, each drawn as below. Each is a default, open to change.

  1. The form is "Step 1 of 2". Ewan sends the application, then sees "Application sent". The card step happens later, from the approval email or Your Memberships.
  2. No vehicle registration on the application. It's for the car park on the day, and Andy doesn't need it to decide.
  3. Date of birth is one date field, the same date input the day ticket uses. It's filled in from his account once saved.
  4. The application shows "Price if approved £20". The booking fee is added at the card step, as with a day ticket, so "Pay £20 and join" stays the ticket price.
  5. "Application sent" uses the amber hourglass mark, not the green tick. He isn't a member yet. The shape and the words carry the difference, not the colour.
  6. The offer card says "Application sent" in place of Apply while he waits, and "Approved · pay to join" once Andy approves. Both link to Your Memberships.
  7. The card says "Applicants need approving". It's one extra line, so Callum knows before he taps Apply that it isn't an instant buy.
  8. The Applications page shows the email address as well as the phone, because that's where Andy will write if he has a question.
  9. Decline opens a reason box inside the row. The reason is optional, and the button says who gets emailed: "Decline and email Priya".
  10. After Approve, the row leaves the list and a green line says Ewan has been emailed a link that runs out on Wed 17 Mar.
  11. The empty Applications page says nothing is waiting and links back to the fishery. The menu link stays, without a count, for as long as the club has a membership that needs approving.
  12. The fishery notice sits inside the Tickets section, above the cards, as the shared .lf-notice. It names the memberships waiting and links to the Applications page. It's only shown to the club's managers.
  13. On Your Memberships, waiting and approved are both in Applications, above Current. "Waiting for the club" has an hourglass and "Approved" has a tick. Both carry the word as well as the icon.
  14. One per club while he waits: the other cards stay open. An application isn't a membership, so while Ewan waits on Junior, D&KLB's Adult still says Buy and Local still says Apply. The first membership he pays for wins. Any other approval then says "You're a member for 2027" in place of Pay, and drops off. The alternative is one open application per club.
  15. No withdraw button in ship 2. Ewan can't cancel an application from the page. If he asks, Andy declines it.

Not drawn, defaults

  • Declined. The application drops off Your Memberships. The "Unfortunately you've not been successful" email carries Andy's reason, and the card says Apply again.
  • After 14 days unpaid. The approval drops off Your Memberships and the card says Apply again, as the agreed rule says.
  • The season fills while he's approved. The row keeps "Approved", but in place of Pay it says "2027 season full". The pay link opens a page that says the same.
  • Andy's email. "Ewan Mackay has applied for D&KLB's Junior membership (under 16)." It has a Review applications button to the Applications page.
