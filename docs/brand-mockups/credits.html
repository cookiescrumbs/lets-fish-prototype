Credits

Let's Fish uses data and services from the following third-party sources. We are grateful to these organisations for making their data available.

UK Lakes Portal

Lake data including names, locations, depths, surface areas, and descriptions is sourced from the UK Lakes Portal, operated by the UK Centre for Ecology & Hydrology (CEH).

Contains Ordnance Survey data. Public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

Geograph Britain and Ireland

Location photographs are sourced from Geograph Britain and Ireland. Individual image attributions are displayed alongside each photograph. Images are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.0 licence.

OpenStreetMap & OpenTopoMap

Map data is provided by OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Data Commons Open Database Licence (ODbL). Map tiles are rendered by OpenTopoMap.
