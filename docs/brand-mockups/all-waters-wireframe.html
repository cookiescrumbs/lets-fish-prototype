All waters — wireframes

Desktop and phone after the de-clutter pass. Grey boxes only; the working hi-fi is all-waters.html. Orange numbers are the changes:

  1. Search folds into the filter row on the phone — a round magnifier that opens search as a sheet. Desktop keeps the search bar in the panel.
  2. "All" pill dropped on both. Two facets don't need a master filter.
  3. Pills trimmed on the phone: same 42px tap height, smaller text, tighter padding, thinner border, no shadow.
  4. Layers becomes a 44px round button on the phone, matching Near me, so the rail is one small stack.
  5. Fewer, smaller pins on the phone: bigger cluster radius, 28px pins.
  6. List pill hides while a card is docked. Dismissing the card brings it back.
